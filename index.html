About MD Haffiz Mahamud

Curiosity, engineering discipline, and a commitment to research that works beyond ideal laboratory conditions.

A Journey of Discovery

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from RUET pursuing research at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and autonomous perception.

My work asks how intelligent systems can remain reliable when visibility is poor, sensor measurements are uncertain, or computational models must carry meaningful guarantees.

I am seeking PhD opportunities where I can develop and evaluate methods that make autonomous systems safer and more dependable.

“I build perception systems that remain reliable when the world becomes uncertain.”— MD Haffiz Mahamud
