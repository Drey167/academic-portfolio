Selected work

2025–26

Severity-Conditioned Object Detection under Fog

Undergraduate thesis developing a PyTorch/YOLOv8 framework that uses a learned severity estimator and feature-wise modulation to adapt detection under changing visibility. The evaluation examines confidence calibration as well as detection accuracy.

Thesis

2026–Now

Certified Amortized Homogenization

Developing a microstructure-conditioned neural-operator framework for conductivity and plane-strain elasticity, targeting rigorous upper and lower effective-property bounds with numerical validation. Manuscript in preparation.

Research

2024

LiDAR–Radar Sensor Fusion for Autonomous Perception

Designed a dual-sensor distance-estimation pipeline for dusty, foggy, and low-light conditions; compared Kalman filtering and weighted fusion approaches to improve robustness and reliability.
